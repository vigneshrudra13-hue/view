Reference Notes

Arena Breakout serves as the primary visual reference for interior environment design and texture development. The game's gritty, military-industrial aesthetic — marked by worn surfaces, layered environmental storytelling, and high-fidelity detail on degraded materials — provides the foundational language for interior texture work. Key takeaways include the handling of concrete, metal oxidation, worn fabric, and mixed-material transitions.
